# Audience Capture: Viewers, Communities and Streamers

A reference for the current build (4 November 2026): the 44 viewer cards in the roster, the 14 communities and the 11 streamers. Rules come from the game code, communities from the Game Database and wording from the English strings.

**Terms:** **Clout** is what viewers earn and what you recruit with. **Cash** pays for Sub Cap raises and actives. **Risk** is Deplatform Risk: at 3 the stream gets deplatformed and pays nothing. **Plays** are the star viewers: 4 in one successful stream wins. A viewer's **action** can be used once per stream.

---

## 2. Communities

A community's **score** is the number of *different* viewer types from that community in chat. Two Loyal Fans count as 1 point; a Loyal Fan and a Normie count as 2. Scores update live during the stream (Community Beats), and each community stops improving at its maximum. At the maximum, its icon shines in Community Beats.

| Community | Game logic | Max | Roster viewers in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core Fanbase** | The regulars. Every 3 points cancel 1 Risk while they're in chat. | No limit | Loyal Fan, Whale, Ineffective Troll, Normie, Gatekeeper, Promo Code User, Generous Donator, Binge Bunny, Die Hard Fan, Evangelist |
| **Staff** | The people the stream pays. Each point gives every Staff viewer a 10% chance to have their Cash cost waived each time they're scored. | 9 (90%) | Producer, Chat Admin, Cybersecurity Engineer, Junior PR Manager, Marketing Manager, Account Manager, PR Manager, Chat Arbitrator, Social Media Manager, Executive PR, Senior Marketing Manager |
| **Content** | Each point gives every viewer *outside* the Core Fanbase a 10% chance to score twice when the stream ends. | 9 (90%) | Mildly Risky Collaborator, Highly Risky Collaborator, Risky Orbiter, Online Scandal, Bigger Star, Precocious Youth, Internet Starpower, Mainstream Celebrity |
| **Techspertise** | If the server goes down (chat over the Sub Cap), the stream may still score: 20% / 40% / 50% at 1 / 2 / 3 points. | 3 (50%) | Cybersecurity Engineer, Bot Detector, Tech Admin |
| **Finance Bros** | Each point gives every viewer a 10% chance to score their Cash reward twice. | 9 (90%) | Paid Sponsorship, Generous Donator, Affiliate, Master of Currency, Billionaire Benefactor |
| **Trolls** | If the stream gets deplatformed (Risk over the limit), each point pays 1 random Clout or Cash. This doesn't apply if you end the stream yourself or the server goes down. | No limit | Ineffective Troll, Influential Troll, Gatekeeper, Hate-Watcher |
| **Doomsdayers** (multiplayer) | After your successful stream, each point gives every viewer in your rivals' next stream a 4% chance to score nothing. | 5 (20%) | None in the current roster |
| **Boomer** | Old money, old opinions. When the stream is scored, each point gives +2 Cash and −1 Clout (fixed, once per stream). | No limit | Master of Currency, Billionaire Benefactor, Executive PR, Big Boomer |
| **Millennial** | Very online, slightly broke. When the stream is scored, each point gives +2 Clout and −1 Cash (fixed, once per stream). | No limit | Senior Marketing Manager |
| **Zoomer** | Chaotic. When the stream is scored, two separate rolls, each with a 20% chance per point: +6 Clout, and −3 Cash. | 4 (80%) | Social Media Manager, Gen Z with Job |
| **Armchair Activists** | All talk. Each point gives every Armchair Activist in chat a 10% chance to score nothing when the stream ends. Other viewers aren't affected. | 5 (50%) | None in the current roster |
| **Clout** | Everyone wants a piece. Each viewer outside the Core Fanbase has a 10% chance per point to bring +1 Clout more, every time they score. | 4 (40%) | Bigger Star, Internet Starpower, Platform Owner, Mainstream Celebrity |
| **Memetics Division** | Reposted forever. Each point gives a 1% chance that the whole stream scores a second time: every viewer and every community reward, all rolled again. | 10 (10%) | Degen Dog, Distraught Cat |
| **Bots** (multiplayer) | When your stream is scored, each Bots viewer in chat has a 10% chance per point to send a Bot into the next player's next stream. A Bot takes a space in chat and earns nothing. At most 6 Bots can wait for one player. | 4 (40%) | Clippo The Clanker |

The community "Clout" shares its name with the Clout resource. They have different icons.

## 3. Streamer characters

Each player picks one streamer, or plays with No Streamer for the classic rules. Passives are always on. Actives are paid in Cash in the shop: the first use costs the listed price, and each later use costs 1 more, up to 12. A cooldown is how many turns the active is unavailable after use. Rival actives only work in multiplayer.
